# RBI/2023-24/55 — Reset of Floating Interest Rate on EMI based Personal Loans

Source: https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasCirculardetails.aspx?id=12529
(Summary of the circular, not verbatim. Fetched via web; consult the RBI PDF for exact wording.)

- Reference: DOR.MCS.REC.32/01.01.003/2023-24
- Date: August 18, 2023
- Addressees: Scheduled commercial banks, regional rural banks, primary (urban) co-operative banks and other regulated lenders
- Subject: Reset of floating interest rate on EMI-based personal loans

## Key provisions
- When the benchmark rate changes, lenders must tell borrowers of the impact on EMI and/or tenor.
- Borrowers must be given the option to switch to a fixed rate, and to extend the tenor or raise the EMI.
- Lenders must send a statement of principal and interest recovered to date, the EMI amount, EMIs remaining, and the annualised rate (APR).
- The rules also apply, with adjustments, to other equated-instalment loans. EBLR-linked loans must follow existing rules and monitor benchmark transmission.
- Effective: applies to existing and new loans; existing loans to be brought in by December 31, 2023, with existing borrowers notified of their options.
